`hero/pool-golden-hour.jpg` (the original dark hero, Phase 1) is kept
and reused as the Final CTA section's background -- its dusk drama
suits a closing moment better than an opening one. `hero/dream-backyard.jpg`
(Phase 1.6) is now the primary hero -- a wide, complete backyard scene
(pool, spa, fire feature, covered lounge, desert landscaping) replacing
the Phase 1.5 hero, which is reused as one of the "Get Inspired" images
instead (`hero/backyard-bright.jpg`).

`categories/outdoor-living.jpg` (v2, Phase 1.6) replaces the Phase 1
fire-pit photo -- a rustic backyard fire pit read noticeably weaker
than its neighboring category tiles once those got bigger; a modern
evening lounge/pool scene reads as premium instead.

No genuine free "backyard putting green" photograph was found on
Unsplash after several searches (the category is scarce in the free
tier) -- `categories/putting-greens.jpg` (v2) is a premium modern
estate with a manicured lawn/spa area instead. Worth revisiting once
real client photography exists for this category.

Replacing any image later: overwrite the file in place (same filename)
or update the path in `src/lib/images/category-imagery.ts` -- no
component code needs to change either way.
